Hunks: the unified formatted-text unit

The mission of a hunk is being one unified internal representation of formatted/tokenized text. All be/bee views produce tokenized hunks which in turn get converted into plain/colored/HTML by renderers. The internal format for hunks is binary TLV. Hunks are defined in libdog's dog/HUNK.h, bound into JS by jab/quickjab as abc.ram("HUNK", cap). A hunk contains:

  1. uri of the hunk (like a Web page, hunk has its URI)
  2. ts timestamp
  3. verb the name of the view producing the hunk
  4. text raw text stream of the hunk
  5. toks a text tokenization as a stream of tok32 entries each packing a 5-bit syntax tag, 2-bit diff side (eq/in/rm), 24-bit end offset, see dog/TOK.h.
