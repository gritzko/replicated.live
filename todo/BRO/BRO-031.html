BRO-031: refresh of the call-less launch view loses its scope

Now:
OPEN

Input

Context

Found 2026-07-18 while pty-verifying the _runSpell context fix (status click composition, pager.js): the LAUNCH view — the one bro was started with, which has no recorded view.call — refreshes by re-deriving a bare verb with no context, so a scoped launch loses its scope on refresh.

  • Repro (pty): jab status ///be/ from the journal root → click into a file → - back to the launch view → R refresh: content becomes the UNSCOPED launch-tree status while the bar still says ///be/: status.
  • Repro 2 (gritzko 2026-07-18, live): jab status in be/, then context-nav spells :./verbs → :./post: bar says refreshed ///be/verbs/post/: status but content is still the WHOLE-tree rows (shared/…, views/…) — the context-change refresh ran the launch verb with the launch scope, not the current context.
  • Bar and content disagree either way — the bar carries the context, the hunks come from the launch scope.

Goals

Refresh must replay the launch view's actual invocation, scope included.

  • R on the launch view re-runs the launch verb IN the current context (status under ///be/verbs/post/ after :./post), matching the bar.
  • Views navigated-to are already correct (the _runSpell fix records view.call.context); only the call-less launch view lacks a recorded call to replay.
  • RULED (gritzko 2026-07-18): status scopes to a sub/dir like all other views — a no-arg status under ///be/verbs/post/ clips to that subtree. The all-views dir-scoping audit is BRO-032.

Constraints

  • Pager stays arg-blind: record and replay the launch invocation (args + context), don't re-derive or parse URIs in the pager; resolution stays with the verbs / resolve_hash.
  • Minimal seam: likely popView/_refresh giving the launch view a synthetic call at startup, not a refresh-time special case.
