CFOLDEmit{Diff,Full} concatenates every visible token — EQ, IN and RM interleaved — into one hunk text, then cfold_overlay_syntax runs HUNKu32bTokenize over that text to recover syntax tags. The text is not a blob of any revision, so the lexer sees dog/tok/LINK.rl:94dog/tok/LINK.rl:77 and BEE-019:33LITE-025:yU: LINK.rl's trailing guard (a letter right after the anchor digits) voids the first ref, which crumbles into prose words, while the second, followed by a real space, fuses as F. Meanwhile the fold already stores a per-token syntax tag lexed on the token's OWN blob at fold time (cfold_tokenize), and the emitters throw it away: cfold_dec_cb does (void)tag and both emit loops pack the neutral 'S' before re-lexing.

Input

Context

  • Site: dog/CFOLD.c cfold_overlay_syntax (was :599) called from cfold_emit_flush on the hunk text; the emitters feed 'S' at :764 and :814.
  • Inherited verbatim from the retired WEAVE.c:1003 (weave_overlay_syntax, "like graf" — DOG-082 port).
  • Seen in bee diff --html index/perma.js: BEE-014:44:P7 (rm) and BEE-019:33:Xc (rm) render as tok-D words while their :P7/:Xc in-side twins get tok-F; door.js:416:tX likewise.
  • Rule (gritzko): tokenize only actual consistent historical BLOBS, never the weave buffer.

Goals

  • Every emitted tok32 carries the tag the fold stored for that token; the IN and RM refs both render as F regardless of what abuts them in the hunk text.
  • No lexer pass over hunk text anywhere in the emit path.

Constraints

  • Repro-first: dog/test/CFOLD06.c folds LINK.rl:77 -> :94 and asserts F on both sides.
  • Existing CFOLD05 byte/side expectations must not move.

WIP

Design decisions

  • Carry the stored tag through cfdec (tag[]) and pack it into outtoks; delete cfold_overlay_syntax, the combined carve and the ext plumbing in cfold_emit_flush.
  • Not the per-side re-lex (EQ+RM / EQ+IN) first proposed: the stored tags are already per-blob lexes, so no mapping is needed.
