MARK-007 MED: past MARK_MAX_REFS=512 refdefs the collector silently stops — links misresolve, no warning

The refdef collector caps at MARK_MAX_REFS 512 (MARK.c:13/:104-118) and SILENTLY stops collecting past it; a reference to a dropped definition then falls back to the implicit form — probe with 600 refdefs: [R599] resolves to ./R599.html instead of its defined p599.html. A silent WRONG link, not even a refusal; ticket-board-scale pages (todo/README) can plausibly reach 512 definitions. From the 2026-07-07 mark/ diagnostic review, orchestrator-verified by probe.
