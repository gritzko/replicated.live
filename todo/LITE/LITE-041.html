Found on /cat/Documentation/power/opp.rst (gritzko, 2026-08-15): dev_pm_opp_add and every other API term rendered as a paragraph with a block-quoted body. reST's DEFINITION LIST (the spec's rule: a one-line term DIRECTLY over an indented block, no blank between) was punted in LITE-037, so the kernel's dominant API-doc idiom fell to the quote path. A term + BLANK + indented body stays a block quote — that is what the spec says, and bonding.rst-style pages keep it.
