MAC: run renders as paragraphs with block-quoted bodies — LITE-037 punted single-letter enumerators "so a sentence opening A. isn't eaten", and the punt is what leaves the indented bodies with a quote bar. The cure is docutils' own: a single-letter enumerator opens a list only when its item shows a BODY or a SIBLING — next line blank, indented, or another same-kind marker — so A. Smith went home. stays prose. The emitter carries the letter as <ol type="a"|"A">, one attribute, so browsers number the items a., b. themselves.
