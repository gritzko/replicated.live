Ruled by gritzko 2026-08-15 on /cat/Documentation/power/power_supply_class.rst: per /wiki/Link a bare prose ref like drivers/power/supply/ds2760_battery.c MUST be recognized and MUST be <a> in rendered HTML. This overturns LITE-037's parity punt ("bare prose refs stay the painted-source side's job"). Recognition stays the TOKENIZER's alone (the LITE-033 ruling): hook.fTokens over the text with ext txt — DOG-037's LINK-bearing TXTT — and each F resolves through refUrl, the same memoized door a painted click rides; a miss stays plain text. Both rendered dialects (.md, .rst) get it, in step.
