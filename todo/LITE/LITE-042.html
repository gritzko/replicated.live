Design decisions

  • Inline markup start/end may neighbor "similar non-ASCII punctuation" (the spec's rule): any char >0x7F passes startOk/afterOk — fullwidth ，。, curly quotes, and CJK prose itself. Liberal past the spec's Unicode-category list, deliberately: zh/it/pt kernel docs are the corpus that bleeds.
  • Line blocks | line render as docutils' div.line-block over div.line, a bare | an empty line — the license-rules.rst <p>|</p> leak gone; the list split around one stays, numbering rides start=.
  • An attribution (--/---/dash flush left) marks a REAL quotation: kept as <blockquote> with <p class="attribution">— author</p>, splitting consecutive quotes per the spec; the LITE-039 lists-only unwrap applies only to attribution-less quotes. power_supply_class.rst's genuine quote stays.
