Goals

RULING (gritzko, chat 2026-07-19): each wt line's fg reflects the ENTRY's freshness.

  • Age < 24h: black #000; then ONE grey shade per each further 24h: #111, #222, ...; top off at #888 for age over a week.
  • Shade = #111 * min(floor(age/24h), 8) — 9 levels, days 0..7 then the 8+ cap.
  • Age = be.now minus the row's ts (d.ts, the wt tip commit ts).
  • The fade paints the row's TEXT (key, leader, time, hashlet, subject); buttons/O-invites keep their own colors.
