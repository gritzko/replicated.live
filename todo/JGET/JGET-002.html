JGET-002 CRIT: jab get has no fast-forward/ancestry gate — silently clobbers committed local commits

A jab get re-syncing from a remote that is BEHIND or DIVERGENT from the local tip silently discards committed local history: it unconditionally appends the incoming tip to the wtlog and resets the worktree, with NO ancestor/fast-forward check anywhere in the seed. Native be get refuses a non-fast-forward (local tip not an ancestor of the incoming tip) and keeps local work. VERIFIED from evidence: case 28 jab wt regressed version B→version A (local commit dropped); case 58 jab wt = peer sibling peer-D (local clone-C clobbered). Part of JGET-001; method Issues.

Design decisions

  • Add the ancestor check at the seed commit-point (before appendWtlog + the

    reset fan-out): if oldTip is set and !dag.isAncestor(k, oldTip, tip) → refuse. A fresh clone (no oldTip) and an exact-equal tip stay no-ops.

    offer a ?back park — out of scope here, refuse is the safe floor).
