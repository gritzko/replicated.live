QJAB-017: io.readdir("dir/") drops the first byte of every entry name

io.readdir (quickjab/io.c::JABCioReaddir) called with a path that ends in / returns every name with its first byte cut off: io.readdir("/home/gritzko/src") gives bee-CODE-074/, but io.readdir("/home/gritzko/src/") gives ee-CODE-074/. This made bee's wts list nothing under SRC_ROOT=/home/gritzko/src/; BEE-080 works around it by stripping the slash in bee, but every other caller is still exposed.

Goals

  • A repro test: io.readdir of d, d/ and d// return the same names, array and callback forms, recursive too.
  • The fix lands where the offset is computed (quickjab or libabc), not by trimming in JS.
