DIS-046 MED: 3-way weave merge fails ("graf err") on content with literal conflict markers

A be get 3-way merge of a file whose CONTENT contains the literal weave conflict markers <<<< |||| >>>> fails with "graf err", leaving the wt copy unmerged — the merge-side analog of DIS-040 / POST-006 (POSTCFLCT false-fires on the same strings). Found updating the JS-027 worktree. Method: Issues.

    sniff: merge failed for js/INDEX.md (graf err) — leaving wt content untouched + merge failures: 1 file(s). The other 4 files weave-merged fine.

  • js/INDEX.md:42 documents WEAVEEmitMerged and literally contains

    `framing conflicts <<<< |||| >>>> ` — the exact sigils the merge writer uses.

  • Path: GRAFMergeWtFile (graf/GET.c:419) → GRAFMergeWtFileTunable
