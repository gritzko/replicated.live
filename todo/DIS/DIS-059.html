WIP

Design decisions

  • attachedBranch reads get rows ONLY (not get/post/patch): posts/patches advance the tip but do NOT change which branch you are ON. This is why status (baselineTip, folds patch) and post (curTip) drifted.
  • Detached iff the recentmost get's query holds a full-sha AND refOf finds no branch; ?#<sha> (sha in the FRAGMENT) is ATTACHED-trunk, ?<sha> (sha in the QUERY) is DETACHED.
  • A detached post writes the cur row ?#<commit.sha> (so curTip advances → next post's parent) but SKIPS advanceRef; attachedBranch (GET-only) keeps the wt detached at the original get — status shows it ahead of the detach point, trunk untouched.
