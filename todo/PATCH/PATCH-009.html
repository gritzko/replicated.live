PATCH-009: whole-tree be patch ?main! TAKE-THEIRS on a DIVERGED sub gitlink (drops our side) instead of a recursive 3-way sub-merge

Now
DONE

A whole-tree be patch be://spot/journal?main! whose be / beagle sub gitlinks have DIVERGED (ours and theirs each carry commits the other lacks, sharing an LCA) resolves the sub by TAKE-THEIRS: it stages put <sub>#theirs and re-GETs the sub at theirs' pin, OVERWRITING ours and DROPPING our side's commits. It should instead RECURSIVELY 3-WAY MERGE the sub (base = LCA, ours = cur, theirs = pin), producing a sub-merge commit that carries BOTH sides, and pin the parent to that merge. The classifier already names the case — SUBS_STALE_DIVERGED ("R4.base and R1 pin are cousins … merge via patch, not a plain bump", sniff/SUBS.h:195) — but the recursion lumps DIVERGED in with BEHIND and treats both as a take-theirs GET. This is the live blocker for the treadmill goal (a genuine two-sided whole-tree merge with be://spot/journal). Method Issues.
