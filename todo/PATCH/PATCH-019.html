Context

The wire arm of the patch fetch leg (be:/ssh:/http(s):, be/verbs/patch/fetchleg.js:89-99) is tested only by the dead-source refusal probe in test/patch/tree-uri — no test ever completes a wire fetch and merges the result. test/patch/fetch-cross-store and fetch-wt-cross-store assert "objects landed" purely by wc -c growth of *.keeper with an unchanged log count — never that the fetched tip actually resolves, that its closure is complete, or that no garbage rode along. Authority-gated remote-ref saving (fetchleg.js:104-107: authority sources save a remote-tracking ref, host-less must not) is asserted in neither direction. The fetch leg itself landed under /todo/PATCH/PATCH-011.

Goals

A hermetic loopback wire-fetch case (serve side is jab upload-pack) whose asserts are semantic: theirs sha resolvable in the local store post-fetch, merged bytes on disk, one patch row; cross-store cases upgraded from byte-growth to tip-resolves + closure-walk asserts; remote-ref probes for both the authority and host-less arms.

Constraints

  • Local-only wire (localhost or a file-duplex transport); tests must not touch the network.
  • Tests only; keeper/idx quirks are worked around as in patchcase.sh, not "fixed" here.
