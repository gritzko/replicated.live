LITE-025 minted BEE-005:1Jz:mJpI — a ron64 BYTE OFFSET plus a 4..10 char EVEN hashlet — and a page full of them reads heavy (gritzko, 2026-08-16). Two cuts, ruled in chat: segment 1 becomes the LINE in the anchored blob (the hashlet already pins the blob, so a line in it is exactly as stable as a byte offset in it — LITE-025:46:zc ruled blob-vs-weave, not byte-vs-line), and the hashlet shrinks to a 2-char minimum, extended ONE char at a time only when it collides in the path's own history OR to get one non-digit into it. BEE-005:58:mJ: legible before AND after minting, and the mint becomes a pure append. The corpus is migrated once by a temporary converter verb, then the old form is forgotten.

Input

Context

The permalink form and its readers/writers, as landed:

  • Form: path:OFF:HASHLET, OFF = ron64 byte offset into the anchored blob (pm.packOffset), HASHLET = ron64 pairs, 12 bits a pair = 3 hex, min 4 chars, EVEN, one non-digit, extended by 2 (LITE-025:19:zc, LITE-025:20:zc; index/perma.js isHashlet hashletHex mintHashlet packOffset).
  • Follow: door.js permaTail/splitRef (segment 2 non-digit decides), door.js seatOf -> index/perma.js follow(partial, offSeg, hash, from) -> land -> anchorOf -> blobsFor (hex-prefix compare over historyOf, EARLIEST wins, then the working blob via blobIdOf) -> tokenAt/tokenNow on one CFOLD pair -> lineCol.
  • Mint: index/hook.js mintRef/mintForeign (ref.path + ":" + pm.packOffset(off) + ":" + h), the LITE-027 topological order (byte offsets in a target shift when its own refs are rewritten), index/mint.js (BEE-016) reusing the same, view/see.js reading pm.byteAt for line bands.
  • Grammar: quickjab/dog/tok/LINK.rl:94 commit = ":" ron64{1,4} ":" (ron64 ron64){2,5} fuses the anchor into the ONE F token; :58:mJ does NOT fuse today (segment 2 needs 4+ even chars), :58:mJpI does. Anchors are capped at 10 chars (DOG-034).

Goals

file.c:58:mJ is the permalink; everything that minted, fused, split or followed the old form does the same for the new one, and one converter run turns the corpus over.

  • Form: path:LINE:HASHLET. LINE = 1-based line in the ANCHORED blob, decimal digits as typed. HASHLET = 2..10 ron64 chars, 6 bits each, a bit-prefix of the blob sha1 (k chars = top 6k bits); grown ONE char at a time until it is unique among the path's other blobs AND carries a non-digit. Segment 2 all-digit = a column, as before.
  • Follow: hashlet -> earliest blob in the path's history whose sha1 top 6k bits match, else the working blob; the line is turned into a byte with pm.byteAt(was, line, 1); the rest of the walk (tokenAt/tokenNow, deleter, notes, lo/hi) is unchanged.
  • Mint (hook, bee mint, foreign): path:LINE:h — the line the ref names in the target image, the hashlet minted for that image's blob id against the path's other blobs. A ref past the end still mints nothing.
  • Grammar (DOG-043): commit = ":" [0-9]{1,10} ":" ron64{2,10} in LINK.rl, so a 2-char or odd hashlet fuses; six includers regenerated.
  • Converter: a TEMPORARY verb bee reperma [--dry-run] <file>... (index/reperma.js, the BEE-016 mint shape) turns every OLD permalink in the named files into the new form TEXTUALLY, with NO index or ODB read: the hashlet is kept — an old pairwise hashlet is bit-for-bit a new one (12 bits a pair = 6 bits a char, big-endian) — and TRIMMED to its shortest prefix (min 2) that carries a non-digit; the ron64 offset is decoded and turned into the LINE it falls on in the file AS IT IS ON DISK NOW (the path found the way the door finds any path:LINE ref — ladder, mount table, fs), lineCol(bytes, off).line. A path no file answers is left and reported. Deleted after the corpus is converted (its own follow-up).
  • Docs and comments follow: README.mkd, door.js, perma.js header, LINK.rl header; core/Link.mkd is gritzko's.

WIP

Design decisions

Ruled by gritzko in chat, 2026-08-16.

  1. LINE, not byte offset, in segment 1 — legibility before and after minting; the hashlet pins the blob, so nothing is lost but the column, which prose links never carried. No :LINE:COL:HASH form (YAGNI; the 10-char anchor cap would eat it anyway).
  2. Hashlet min 2, step 1, bit-prefix compare: 6 bits a char, k chars = the sha1's top 6k bits (BigInt shift), so odd lengths mean something. Extend ONLY on collision in the path's own history or to obtain a non-digit. Both hashletBits(h) -> { bits, val } and mintHashlet(sha, others) live in index/perma.js, ONE file owning both directions (LITE-026:102:xo stands).
  3. "First" = EARLIEST in the path's history order, then the working blob (LITE-025:22:zc stands): a younger blob sharing the prefix was minted a longer hashlet of its own.
  4. The mint order of LITE-027 STAYS: line numbers no longer shift when a target's own refs are rewritten, but its blob id does, so a carrier still needs its target final.
  5. NO dual reader. After the flip splitRef reads segment 1 as digits only; an old :1Jz:mJpI splits as no anchor at all (segment 1 is not all-digit) and lands on the file. The converter is the migration; committed history keeps dead old links, accepted.
  6. The converter tells OLD from NEW by segment 1: a non-digit ron64 offset is old; an ALL-DIGIT old offset (:5:mJpI, offset < 10) is indistinguishable from a new-form line and is reported, never guessed. gritzko 2026-08-16: the conversion is offset -> decimal line against the CURRENT file bytes plus hashlet TRIM to 2 chars (3+ only to get a non-digit) — no re-mint, no uniqueness check, no db read: no file here has a history long enough to need it, and the corpus was minted a day ago.
  7. Grammar in the SAME move (DOG-043) — without it a 2-char hashlet is not an F token and the door never sees it; the temporary min-4 compat was weighed and dropped, it forfeits half the point.
