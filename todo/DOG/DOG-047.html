WHIFFKeyPack keys are hashlet60:60|type:4 — the type in the LS nibble, so every type's rows interleave uniformly across the whole keyspace and a fat family pollutes every page. Flip it: type:4|payload:60. Each kind then owns one contiguous 1/16 range, pages stay homogeneous, a range scan can take a whole kind, and BEE-063's SYM bulk (kind 1111) sorts last instead of everywhere. Every wh128 lane is DERIVED and its only reader is its own dog (bee reads only bee's), so the break costs a rebuild behind a format bump, never an in-place migration.

Input

Context

  • WHIFF.h:138:hv WHIFFKeyPack is the canonical key; the LIVE consumers are be and its fork bee alone (gritzko, 2026-08-24) — the abc.index wh128 lanes, the ULOG sidecar, the dog/git pack-log emit (PIDX.h PIDXEntry). keeper, graf and sniff are phased out; WHIFF.h's prose still names them and that archaeology goes with this change.
  • bee mirrors the convention in JS: index/index.js:42:revKey, :44:hlKey, index/kv.js:37:packKey; INDEXES.mkd states "the kind nibble is the LOW 4 bits of every key".
  • BEE-063 adds ~10x the rows of every other bee kind; the LS-nibble layout puts that bulk on every page of the shared stack, which is what surfaced this.

Goals

  • The wh64 LAYOUT itself flips (gritzko, 2026-08-24): off:40|id:20|type:4 becomes type:4|off:40|id:20 — the shifts/masks in WHIFF.h, wh64Pack/Type/Id/Off, and the file-top layout prose.
  • WHIFFKeyPack(type, hashlet60) then composes as (type << 60) | hashlet60 for free (hashlet spans off+id as today); WHIFFKeyType reads the TOP nibble, WHIFFKeyHashlet the low 60; the WHIFF.h key-section comment and INDEX.md line follow.
  • Every live consumer re-keys through the accessors, none hand-packs: the abc.index wh128 lanes, the ULOG sidecar index, PIDX's entry builder and its tests; bee's JS mirrors follow in BEE-063.
  • Each consumer's on-disk lane takes a format/extension bump and rebuilds lazily from its source of truth (packs, commits, the log) — extension is the format, an old file is swept.
  • VAL nibbles are untouched in MEANING (vnib stays a nibble beside a 60-bit payload), but any val built via wh64Pack moves with the layout; per-consumer val layouts that never touch wh64 accessors stay.

Constraints

  • No dual-format readers, no version headers: sweep and rebuild is the whole migration story.
  • A reader that folded SEVERAL kinds off ONE prefix span now runs one span per kind (bee's loadPath REV zip, the kv HEAD+YAML block). The convention binds KEY lanes; a per-lane catalogue may deliberately stay — bee's lite lane migrates in BEE-063, bee's kv lane has no fat family and its block scan is why it may keep the LS nibble.

WIP

Design decisions

  • (open) whether bee's kv lane follows at all — splitting its one-block scan in two buys nothing there; gritzko rules.
  • Type 1111 sorting LAST is now a property worth assigning deliberately: the fattest family gets F, marks and small families keep low values.
